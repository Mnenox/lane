## How to apply / 如何申请

1. Open an issue titled `Commercial License Request` in the repository, or contact the
   licensor via the contact channel listed in the repository profile;

2. Describe your organization, intended use, scope (products / seats / duration);

3. The licensor replies with terms and pricing; a signed grant constitutes the commercial license.
