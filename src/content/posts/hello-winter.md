This little post is a scheduled-publishing demonstration: it stays hidden until the first morning of winter, then appears on the wall by itself.

Two safety nets keep the promise: a cloud rebuild every 30 minutes, and minute-level punctuality from the lane desktop client whenever your computer is on.

![A lighthouse at the end of the pier, like a postcard waiting to arrive](../../assets/seaside-postcard.jpg)

See you on the wall.
